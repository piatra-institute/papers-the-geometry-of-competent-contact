## Bibliography

Amari, S., and Nagaoka, H. (2000). *Methods of Information Geometry* (D. Harada, Trans.). Translations of Mathematical Monographs 191. American Mathematical Society and Oxford University Press. — the simplex-to-sphere isometry and the Fisher-Rao distance formula (§2). Verified AMS mmono/191.

Ay, N., Jost, J., Lê, H. V., and Schwachhöfer, L. (2017). *Information Geometry*. Ergebnisse der Mathematik und ihrer Grenzgebiete 64. Springer. — general-manifold extension of the Fisher-metric uniqueness (§2). Verified doi:10.1007/978-3-319-56478-4.

Åström, K. J. (1965). Optimal control of Markov processes with incomplete state information. *Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications*, 10(1), 174–205. — the belief as sufficient statistic for control (§2). Verified doi:10.1016/0022-247X(65)90154-X. (Note: gate-invisible lead surname due to the Å; real and correctly attributed.)

Blackwell, D. (1953). Equivalent comparisons of experiments. *The Annals of Mathematical Statistics*, 24(2), 265–272. — the sufficiency order on experiments; preferred in every decision problem (§3, §5). Verified doi:10.1214/aoms/1177729032.

Chentsov, N. N. (1982). *Statistical Decision Rules and Optimal Inference* (Translations of Mathematical Monographs 53). American Mathematical Society. (Original work published 1972.) — finite-case Fisher-Rao uniqueness under sufficient statistics (§2). Verified AMS mmono/53.

Cover, T. M., and Thomas, J. A. (2006). *Elements of Information Theory* (2nd ed.). Wiley-Interscience. — mutual information and KL as the information currency (§4). Verified Wiley doi:10.1002/047174882X.

Dreyfus, H. L. (2002). Intelligence without representation: Merleau-Ponty's critique of mental representation. *Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences*, 1(4), 367–383. — "maximal grip" as the tendency toward an optimal perceptual hold (§1, §6). Verified doi:10.1023/A:1021351606209. Phrase is Dreyfus's gloss of Merleau-Ponty, attributed as such.

Friston, K., Rigoli, F., Ognibene, D., Mathys, C., Fitzgerald, T., and Pezzulo, G. (2015). Active inference and epistemic value. *Cognitive Neuroscience*, 6(4), 187–214. — the epistemic (information-gain) term in expected free energy (§5). Verified doi:10.1080/17588928.2015.1020053.

Gibson, J. J. (1979). *The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception*. Houghton Mifflin. — affordances; perception as active information pickup (§1). Verified.

Gottlieb, J., Oudeyer, P.-Y., Lopes, M., and Baranes, A. (2013). Information-seeking, curiosity, and attention: Computational and neural mechanisms. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 17(11), 585–593. — active sensing as information gain (§4). Verified doi:10.1016/j.tics.2013.09.001.

Itti, L., and Baldi, P. (2009). Bayesian surprise attracts human attention. *Vision Research*, 49(10), 1295–1306. — Bayesian surprise = KL from prior to posterior, the movement of belief (§4). Verified doi:10.1016/j.visres.2008.09.007.

Kaelbling, L. P., Littman, M. L., and Cassandra, A. R. (1998). Planning and acting in partially observable stochastic domains. *Artificial Intelligence*, 101(1–2), 99–134. — the POMDP as a belief-state MDP on the simplex (§2). Verified doi:10.1016/S0004-3702(98)00023-X.

Klyubin, A. S., Polani, D., and Nehaniv, C. L. (2005). Empowerment: A universal agent-centric measure of control. In *Proceedings of the 2005 IEEE Congress on Evolutionary Computation* (Vol. 1, pp. 128–135). IEEE. — empowerment as channel capacity of the action-to-sensor channel (§5). Verified doi:10.1109/CEC.2005.1554676.

Kriegeskorte, N., and Kievit, R. A. (2013). Representational geometry: Integrating cognition, computation, and the brain. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 17(8), 401–412. — task-relevant separability of representations (§4). Verified doi:10.1016/j.tics.2013.06.007.

Kriegeskorte, N., Mur, M., and Bandettini, P. A. (2008). Representational similarity analysis: Connecting the branches of systems neuroscience. *Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience*, 2, 4. — the representational dissimilarity matrix (§4). Verified doi:10.3389/neuro.06.004.2008.

Lindley, D. V. (1956). On a measure of the information provided by an experiment. *The Annals of Mathematical Statistics*, 27(4), 986–1005. — expected information as expected posterior entropy reduction (§4). Verified doi:10.1214/aoms/1177728069.

Merleau-Ponty, M. (2012). *Phenomenology of Perception* (D. A. Landes, Trans.). Routledge. (Original work published 1945.) — perception tends toward an optimal hold; the body geared into the world (§1, §6). Verified Routledge, Landes translation.
